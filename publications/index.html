  1. Preprint
    From Apparent Search Gains to Selection Failures: Auditing Test-Time Inference in Structured Workflow Planning
    Zhitong Chen, Weifeng Zhang, Kai Yin, Zhiyuan Wang, Ali Mostafavi, and James Caverlee
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2611.00579, 2026
    Under review
    Abs arXiv Bib PDF Code

    Audits test-time inference by separating candidate discovery, structural validation, and final selection. Prompt-matched comparisons reveal why finding a correct workflow does not guarantee selecting it.

  2. EMNLP 2026 Findings
    DisasterBench: Benchmarking LLM Planning under Typed Tool Interface Constraints
    Zhitong Chen*, Kai Yin*, Weifeng Zhang, Zhiyuan Wang, Xiangjue Dong, Chengkai Liu, Zhewei Liu, Yiming Xiao, Ali Mostafavi, and James Caverlee
    In Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EMNLP 2026, 2026
    Abs DOI Bib PDF Code

    Evaluates interface-constrained tool-use planning with 233 tasks and 26 tools. First-Point-of-Failure analysis distinguishes tool selection, parameter binding, and dependency errors.

  3. ACL 2026 Findings
    DisastQA: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Evaluating Question Answering in Disaster Management
    Zhitong Chen*, Kai Yin*, Xiangjue Dong, Chengkai Liu, Xiangpeng Li, Bo Li, Junwei Ma, Yiming Xiao, Ali Mostafavi, and James Caverlee
    In Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: ACL 2026, 2026
    Abs DOI Bib PDF Code

    Evaluates disaster question answering with 3,000 expert-annotated questions across eight disaster types. Multiple-choice and open-ended tasks examine factual completeness under different evidence conditions.

  4. AAAI Symposium
    FloodSQL-Bench: A Retrieval-Augmented Benchmark for Geospatially-Grounded Text-to-SQL
    Hanzhou Liu, Kai Yin, Zhitong Chen, Chenyue Liu, and Ali Mostafavi
    In Proceedings of the AAAI Symposium Series, 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib PDF

    A retrieval-augmented text-to-SQL benchmark for flood management. Heterogeneous social, infrastructure, and hazard data support key-based, spatial, and hybrid joins, enabling evaluation of geospatially grounded database reasoning.

  5. Preprint
    DisastRAG: A Multi-Source Disaster Information Integration and Access System Based on Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models
    Bo Li, Zhitong Chen, Kai Yin, Junwei Ma, Yiming Xiao, and Ali Mostafavi
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2605.05210, 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib PDF

    A multi-source disaster information system connecting document retrieval, structured disaster records, and external web fallback. Query understanding and strategy routing select evidence-access pathways for response generation.
