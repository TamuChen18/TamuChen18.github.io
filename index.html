About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Texas A&M University, advised by Prof. Ali Mostafavi. I also collaborate with Prof. James Caverlee.

My research focuses on LLM agents, tool-use planning, and evaluation. I study how language models compose tools into valid workflows, reason with imperfect evidence, and select reliable answers at inference time. My broader interests include agent learning, post-training, and reinforcement learning.

My recent work includes auditing test-time inference and candidate selection, DisasterBench for interface-constrained tool planning, and DisastQA for evidence-grounded question answering.

Open to Summer 2027 research internships.
I am interested in opportunities in LLM agents, planning, and evaluation. Let’s connect.
