I am a Ph.D. student in **Computer Science** at **Texas A&M University**, advised by [Prof. Ali Mostafavi](https://people.engr.tamu.edu/amostafavi/index.html). I also collaborate with [Prof. James Caverlee](https://people.engr.tamu.edu/caverlee/).

My research focuses on **Large Language Model (LLM) Agents**, particularly **tool use, planning, and evaluation**. I am interested in understanding how language models make decisions, coordinate tool interactions, and solve complex tasks. I am also exploring **LLM post-training and reinforcement learning** to improve agentic reasoning and decision-making.

Previously, I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Texas A&M University in December 2025.

## Research Interests

My research interests include:

- **LLM Agents:** Tool use, multi-step planning, and autonomous decision-making.
- **LLM Evaluation:** Benchmarks, agentic workflows, and failure analysis.
- **LLM Post-training:** Reinforcement learning, policy optimization, and distillation.
- **Agentic AI Applications:** Reliable LLM-based systems for real-world decision support.

## Selected Research

### DisasterBench
**Findings of EMNLP 2026 · First Author**

A benchmark for evaluating large language model agents on tool-use planning and scientific workflows in disaster-related domains.

### DisastQA
**Findings of ACL 2026 · First Author**

A benchmark for evaluating the disaster-related knowledge and reasoning capabilities of large language models.

### From Apparent Search Gains to Selection Failures
**arXiv Preprint · Under Review**

An empirical investigation of search strategies for LLM reasoning, examining the gap between generating correct candidate solutions and reliably selecting them.
