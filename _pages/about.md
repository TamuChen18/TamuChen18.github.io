I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student at [Texas A&M University](https://www.tamu.edu/), advised by [Prof. Ali Mostafavi](https://people.engr.tamu.edu/amostafavi/index.html). I also collaborate with [Prof. James Caverlee](https://people.engr.tamu.edu/caverlee/).

My research focuses on **LLM agents**, with an emphasis on **tool-use planning, agent evaluation, and decision-making**. I am particularly interested in understanding the limitations of current agentic systems and improving their reliability through **post-training and reinforcement learning**.

I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Texas A&M University in 2025.

## Research Interests

**LLM Agents** · **Tool Use & Planning** · **LLM Evaluation** · **Post-training & Reinforcement Learning**
