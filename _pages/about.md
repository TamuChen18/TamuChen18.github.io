I am a Ph.D. student in **Computer Science** at **Texas A&M University**, advised by [Prof. Ali Mostafavi](https://people.engr.tamu.edu/amostafavi/index.html). I also collaborate with [Prof. James Caverlee](https://people.engr.tamu.edu/caverlee/).

My research focuses on **LLM agents, tool-use planning, and evaluation**. I study how language models compose tools into valid workflows, reason under imperfect evidence, and select reliable answers at inference time. My broader interests include **agent learning, post-training, and reinforcement learning**.

I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Texas A&M University in December 2025.

**I am seeking Summer 2027 research internships in LLM agents, planning, and evaluation.** Please [get in touch](mailto:zhitong.chen18@tamu.edu) if there is a potential fit.

## Research

- **Test-time inference and selection.** Auditing candidate discovery, structural validation, and final selection to understand where search gains are lost.
- **Tool-use planning.** Evaluating tool selection, parameter binding, and dependency consistency in structured workflows with DisasterBench.
- **Evidence-grounded evaluation.** Studying question answering with clean, incomplete, and noisy evidence through DisastQA.
