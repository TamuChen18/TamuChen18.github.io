Reasoning & Planning

TEST-TIME INFERENCE

From Apparent Search Gains to Selection Failures

Auditing Test-Time Inference in Structured Workflow Planning

Preprint · Under review · 2026

An audit of why discovering a correct workflow does not guarantee selecting it.

  • Separates inference into candidate discovery, structural validation, and final selection.
  • Compares independent sampling and search configurations under controlled inference budgets.
  • Finds that majority voting can miss correct candidates already present in a pool.
  • Examines verifier failures and recommends reporting discovery and selection separately.

TOOL-USE PLANNING

DisasterBench

Benchmarking LLM Planning under Typed Tool Interface Constraints

EMNLP 2026

A benchmark for composing disaster-response tools into interface-valid workflows.

  • Introduces 233 tasks and 26 tools for structured workflow planning.
  • Tests tool choice, parameter binding, and dependency propagation under typed interfaces.
  • Proposes First-Point-of-Failure (FPoF) to distinguish root errors from cascading failures.
  • Analyzes how model capacity and reasoning strategies affect reliable tool coordination.

Evidence-Grounded Evaluation

EVIDENCE-GROUNDED EVALUATION

DisastQA

A Comprehensive Benchmark for Evaluating Question Answering in Disaster Management

ACL 2026

A disaster QA benchmark examining factual completeness under clean and imperfect evidence.

  • Provides 3,000 questions: 2,000 multiple-choice and 1,000 open-ended, across eight disaster types.
  • Uses a human–LLM collaboration pipeline with expert verification and stratified sampling.
  • Compares Base, Golden, and Mix evidence settings to examine knowledge and evidence use.
  • Introduces human-verified keypoint-based evaluation for factual completeness in open-ended answers.

GEOSPATIAL DATABASE REASONING

FloodSQL-Bench

A Retrieval-Augmented Benchmark for Geospatially-Grounded Text-to-SQL

AAAI Symposium · 2026

A Text-to-SQL benchmark for geographically grounded flood-management questions.

  • Connects heterogeneous social, infrastructure, and hazard datasets.
  • Covers key-based, spatial, and hybrid joins across multiple tables.
  • Evaluates language models under shared retrieval-augmented settings and varying difficulty.
  • Provides a domain-grounded testbed for multi-table and geospatial database reasoning.

RETRIEVAL-AUGMENTED GENERATION

DisastRAG

A Multi-Source Disaster Information Integration and Access System Based on Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models

Preprint · 2026

A multi-source system for accessing documents, structured disaster records, and external information.

  • Combines document retrieval, relational data access, and web fallback.
  • Uses query understanding and strategy routing to select evidence pathways.
  • Integrates response generation and contextual memory within a unified information system.
  • Evaluates retrieval configurations on multiple-choice and open-ended disaster information tasks.
